The Problem

Every sponsor we talk to has the same spreadsheet problem.

Your portfolio lives in Yardi, Argus, Excel, email, and Dropbox. Your analyst spends 15 hours a week manually abstracting leases. Your investor reports take 2 days to compile. And nobody can answer "what's happening across the portfolio" without opening 7 files.

The lease abstraction tax

Every acquisition, refi, or disposition requires 3–4 hours of manual lease review per property. A 20-asset sponsor burns 200+ analyst hours annually on work that a system should handle.

The comp research treadmill

Your team pulls sales and lease comps from 4 different sources, normalizes them in Excel, and rebuilds the analysis every quarter. The data exists. The system doesn't.

The investor reporting scramble

Quarterly distributions require 2 days of analyst time: pulling performance data, writing narrative, building charts, checking for errors. Every quarter. Every property.

The portfolio blind spot

No single view of critical dates, lease expirations, tenant health, or market position. Decisions are reactive because visibility is fragmented.
